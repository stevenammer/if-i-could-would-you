# How an issue gets made

I don't hide that AI helps me write this newsletter. The whole point of Issue 1 is that I tried to outsource my own writing, and the honest follow-up is to show you what that looks like when it's not a polished story.

## What's here

- [`issue-02-ai-first-draft.md`](issue-02-ai-first-draft.md) is the first draft an AI wrote for an Issue 2 idea I ended up dropping. Its first line says it hasn't been reviewed by me, because it hadn't been.
- [`issue-02-ai-notes-for-steven.md`](issue-02-ai-notes-for-steven.md) is the AI's own list of assumptions, which claims it could source, which it couldn't verify, and which lines it thought least sounded like me. I like this one best. It's a machine telling me where it was guessing.
- [`../issues/issue-02.md`](../issues/issue-02.md) is what I actually published.

## These are not two versions of the same issue

I expected to show you a clean "AI draft, then my edits." That's not what the files say, and I'd rather tell you what's true.

- The AI's first draft was written for a different idea: running my own Voice Workshop prompt on myself and seeing what it learned about my voice.
- The issue I actually published threw that idea out. It opens with me saying last week was a complete and utter disaster, decides to table the writing topic, and goes back to a question I'd wanted to answer from the start: what is actually happening when you type something into an AI chat window and hit send?
- So the draft and the published issue share a format and a couple of section headings (one heading, "Something to watch on the throne", survives word for word), and almost nothing else. I compared them sentence by sentence: of the draft's 85 sentences, 76 are less than half similar to anything in the published issue.
- The published issue is also about twice as long, roughly 2,890 words against 1,470.

What that shows is a pivot. The draft is the thing I decided not to publish. What I can't show you from these files is the steps in between, because that rewriting happened in conversations I haven't added yet.

## What's not here yet

The middle. The back and forth, my corrections, the times I threw something out. That lives in my chat history. I'm going to add a cleaned-up version of it, with anything private taken out. Until then, treat this folder as the beginning and the end of the process, not the whole thing.
