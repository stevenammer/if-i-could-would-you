# If I Could, Would You?

A weekly newsletter where I find out what I can do with AI, then find out if I can teach it in a way that inspires other people to try.

**[Read and subscribe on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/if-i-could-would-you%3F-7521263683642056705/)**

I'm not in IT. I have an associate's degree that took me ten years and fifteen years on phone sales floors. I started taking AI seriously about a year ago, and I'm learning in public, so some of this is rough on purpose. If you're an expert and I'm steering people wrong, please tell me.

This repo is the workshop behind the newsletter. The newsletter is where you read it. This is where you can take it apart.

I build most of this with AI agents. I write the specs, try to break what comes back, and decide what's safe to ship. These are the other places that shows up:

- [Interview Coach](https://github.com/stevenammer/interview-coach): an open-source app for practicing interviews with AI feedback.
- [Before the Vibes](https://github.com/stevenammer/before-the-vibes): my C assignments from 2013 and 2014, bugs and all, with an honest review.
