If I Could, Would You?Issue #4: Context Management

The Context Vision Quest

Ask an AI something. Get an answer that could have been written for anyone. Then let an AI interview you in a chat, get a context file you own, and ask the same thing again.

The two answers come from a real AI (Google's Gemini) running on a small budget I set. What you type in steps 1, 2 and 5 is sent to it so it can answer and ask its next question. If you use the Talk button, that short recording is sent to the same AI to be written down. Nothing is saved on my side, and there are no accounts. If the daily budget runs out, you can still paste the interview prompt into your own AI, and the exports still work.

Step 1Ask your AI something

Step 2The interview

It's a chat. The AI asks one question at a time and you answer however you like. Skip anything you don't want to share. Typing a pain? Hit Talk and just say it. Ramble. It writes it down.

Answer a few and the "Write my file" button wakes up. Your answers go to the AI so it can ask the next question. Nothing is saved.

Steal the interview prompt and run it in your own AI instead
