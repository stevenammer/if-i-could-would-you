Talking to your AI with Wispr Flow

A plain guide to getting it on your phone. It's the tool I mention in Issue #4 for answering the context interview out loud. I'm not affiliated with Wispr, and the steps below come from their own help pages, which I link at each step. Phones and apps change, so if something doesn't match, trust their page over mine.

Checked November 18, 2026.

Before you install

  • It needs permissions that are a big deal. On iPhone it's a keyboard with Full Access. On Android it's a service that can see and type into other apps. Read what you're agreeing to and decide if you're comfortable with it.
  • Don't talk your passwords, card numbers or other people's private details into it. That goes for any AI tool.
  • Work phone? Check with your company first. Their rules about outside apps come before my guide.

iPhone

  1. Install Wispr Flow from the App Store and open it once. The keyboard doesn't show up in your settings until you've opened the app.
  2. Open Settings, then General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, and pick Wispr Flow under Third-Party Keyboards.
  3. Tap Wispr Flow in that list and turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard needs it to work.
  4. Tap the microphone in the app once and allow the microphone when iPhone asks. If you said no earlier, turn it on in Settings, because iPhone won't ask again.
  5. Open any text box, switch to the Flow keyboard (the globe key), tap the button and talk.

Their page: Set up the Flow keyboard on iPhone.

Android

  1. You need Android 13 or newer. Check their requirements page first, because that alone doesn't guarantee it's in your Play Store.
  2. Install Wispr Flow from Google Play and open it.
  3. Allow the three things it asks for: the microphone, "Display over other apps", and the Accessibility service. The Accessibility one is how it types into other apps, and it's the one to think about before you say yes.
  4. If your phone has a work profile, install it in your personal profile. It can't switch on the Accessibility service from a work profile.
  5. Open a text box in any app, tap the little Flow button when it appears and talk.

Their page: Install Wispr Flow on Android.

Using it for the context interview

Open your AI chat, paste in the interview prompt from the Context Vision Quest, and answer each question by talking. Say it the way you'd say it to a friend. If you remember something later, just say it then. You're not writing anything, you're dumping it and letting the AI organize it.

Then read the file it gives you back before you save it anywhere. Talking is fast, and it'll pick up things you'd never have typed.
