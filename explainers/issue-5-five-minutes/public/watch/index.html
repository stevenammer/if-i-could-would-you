The Story of the Three Little Pigs

L. Leslie Brooke's 1904 picture book, brought to life as a read-along. About 5 minutes.

A first shareable draft. The narration is a labeled AI placeholder voice; a real reader will record it.

The book: L. Leslie Brooke, The Story of the Three Little Pigs (Frederick Warne, 1904), public domain. Page scans: Library of Congress, Rare Book and Special Collections Division, which lists its Children's Book Selections as free to use and reuse. The retelling is gentler than the 1904 original: nobody gets eaten.

How it was made: the pages were cut into puppets and animated by AI coding agents (Claude Code and Codex). The black and white pages were colored and eight action moments animated with Google Flow, using these same public-domain pages as the starting frames. Music and sound effects were synthesized for this project.
