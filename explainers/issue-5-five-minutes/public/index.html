This is a replay of the real terminal session where I handed my plan to a coding agent and tried to animate a 1904 picture book for my wife. No shell runs here and nothing calls an AI. You press the button, my real prompt gets typed, and you watch what came back, sped way up.

What's real here: every blue prompt is copied from my session, word for word, except two names swapped for privacy and one word bleeped. Agent output is shortened from the actual session, build logs and commit history; anything tagged yellow is a summary, not a quote. The pictures are real frames from the real drafts.

Why this book: L. Leslie Brooke's The Story of the Three Little Pigs (1904) is public domain. The scans come from the Library of Congress, Rare Book and Special Collections Division, which lists its Children's Book Selections as free to use and reuse. The voice is a labeled AI placeholder until the real narrator records it. A few action shots were made with Google Flow from those same public-domain pages.
