Try this: Run it with the goal at 8. It succeeds in a few passes. Now drag the goal down to 2: the checker is never satisfied, so the only things that stop it are the off ramps. Turn off max tries and it runs until the demo’s own emergency stop at 20 passes. Turn on human approval and you are the off ramp. This is a teaching simulation with pre-written attempts, not a real model or a recording of one.
